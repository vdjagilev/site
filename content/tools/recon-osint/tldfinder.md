## Description

Developed by ProjectDiscovery, tldfinder discovers registered top-level domains (TLDs) and related brand domains for a given target name. It probes hundreds of gTLDs and ccTLDs to surface forgotten assets, international subsidiaries, and potential brand impersonation domains.
