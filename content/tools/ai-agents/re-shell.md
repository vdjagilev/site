## Description

`re-shell` powers autonomous hardware reverse engineering by enabling AI agents (e.g., Claude Opus) to interactively issue commands, inspect raw device memory, reverse proprietary USB/HID protocols, and patch peripheral firmware. Developed by Chaz Schlarp during security research across consumer peripherals.
